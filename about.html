Who We Are

More than a design studio.

At Avani Concepts & Consulting, we believe that great design goes beyond aesthetics. It shapes the way people live, work and experience their surroundings.


Our work brings together creative design and practical consulting to help clients make informed decisions about their spaces, projects and requirements.


From interior design and 3D visualization to construction, real estate and financial consulting, our multidisciplinary approach allows us to look at the larger picture behind every requirement.

Our Design Philosophy

Spaces that feel considered.

Our interior work is rooted in creating spaces that are elegant, functional and personal.

We carefully balance contemporary design principles with timeless elements, paying attention to space planning, lighting, materials, proportions and the way a space will ultimately be experienced.

The objective is not simply to make a room look good, but to create an environment that works for the people using it.

Design Perspective

Making compact spaces feel expansive.

In a recent residential concept, the objective was to transform a compact urban home into an open, calming environment.

The approach focused on optimizing the existing layout, improving the perception of natural light and introducing neutral tones to create a stronger sense of space.

The result was a refined environment that balanced functionality with a contemporary and comfortable aesthetic.
